Search several engines at once. Get a summary written on your device. Ask any page a question — it has already read it.

Every search, four engines deep

Most browsers hand your query to one company. This one asks DuckDuckGo, Bing, Brave and Startpage, merges what comes back, drops the duplicates and tells you which engine found what.

◈

An overview you can check

A short summary of what the results actually say, with the pages it drew on listed underneath. Tap any of them to read the source yourself.

▤

Results first, always

Results appear the moment an engine answers. The summary fills in beneath them — it never makes you wait, and you can stop it at any time.

◎

The tabs you expect

Web, Images, Videos, News, Forums, Books, Flights and Finance. A search that finds nothing widens itself instead of dead-ending.

Ask the page anything

Open any site and tap the orb. Before you have typed a word, the browser has read the page — the publisher's own text, and what is on your screen right now.

It reads the page. Headings, article text, author and date — whatever the site publishes.

It looks at the screen. Text inside images, charts and photos, recognised on your device with Apple's Vision framework.

You just ask. Summarise it, pull out the key points, explain it simply, or question what it claims.

Screenshots are analysed on device and never stored or uploaded

Offline is not a fallback here

The language model ships inside the app. There is no first-run download, no server of ours, and no account. On a plane, on the underground, on a dead connection — the AI still works. Only fetching pages needs the internet.

Bring your own model instead, if you prefer. Connect Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek or Qwen with your own API key — or point the browser at a server you run. The app always says which one is answering, and switching back to on-device takes one tap.

What it does not do

Collect your dataThere is no backend. Nothing about your browsing reaches us, because there is nowhere for it to go.
Track youNo analytics, no advertising SDKs, no identifiers.
Require an accountInstall it and search. That is the whole setup.
Send pages to a serverSummaries and answers are generated on your phone — unless you connect a cloud model yourself, and the app tells you when you have.
